Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026

Nazej is a parking voucher log. It stores what you type and the photos you choose on this device. Nazej has no account server and does not upload your garage names, bay photos, notes, or vouchers.

What stays on the device

  • Parking sessions: garage, bay, level, vehicle note, start, end, hourly rate, fee, and notes.
  • Bay photos you take or import, copied into the app’s private folder.
  • PNG voucher pages generated from those sessions.
  • A local one-tap account id, Park Credit balance, and purchase records needed to avoid granting the same Apple transaction twice.
  • A device notification token if you allow notifications. It stays on this device. Nazej does not send it to a server.

Permissions

Camera is requested only after you tap Take Photo or Add Spot Photo, to capture a bay. Photos read access is requested only after you tap Choose Photo. Adding to Photos is requested only after you tap Save Image. The microphone and speech recognition are requested only after you tap Start Dictation. You confirm the transcript before it fills a field. If you decline, you can keep typing.

After you open the main screen, Nazej may ask for notification permission with the system dialog so a live meter can remind you about the next 15-minute tariff tier. Nazej does not request tracking and does not read the advertising identifier.

Purchases

Park Credits are consumable in-app purchases sold by Apple. Apple processes the payment. Credits are stored on this device. Nazej does not offer Restore Purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple. If Apple revokes a purchase, Nazej removes only the unused part of that grant. Vouchers already created stay. Clear All Data removes the current balance and then issues a new starter balance of 3 Park Credits. Delete Account removes the balance with the account. Deleting the app removes the local balance.

Account

One-Tap Login creates a local session. It is not Sign in with Apple and it does not use a password. Log Out clears the session and keeps your records. Delete Account erases the local account and the data it stored, inside the app.
