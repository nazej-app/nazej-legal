User Agreement

Effective 21 October 2026

Nazej lets you record a parking stop and generate a PNG voucher on this device. The fee shown is a calculation from the duration and the hourly rate you enter, using 15-minute blocks. Nazej does not reserve a bay, predict open spaces, navigate, or pay a garage.

Your records

You can save a draft without a photo. A preview does not spend Park Credits. Generating a voucher spends 1 Park Credit only after the PNG files are stored. If generation fails or you cancel, the credit is not spent.

Credits

New local accounts receive 3 Park Credits on the device. Additional credits are Apple consumable purchases. Prices and names are shown by Apple on the payment sheet. Nazej does not offer Restore Purchases. Clear All Data removes the current balance and then issues a new starter balance of 3 Park Credits. Delete Account removes the local account and its balance. The next One-Tap Login is a new local account and receives its own starter balance of 3. Deleting the app removes the local balance.

License

You receive a personal license to use Nazej. Do not misuse the camera, photo library, or microphone access. You are responsible for the garage names, rates, and notes you enter.

Account

One-Tap Login is the only sign-in. Log Out keeps data. Delete Account removes the local account and its data inside the app.
